# Sing'jar Wards

## Marble Ward

This is where the majority of the government buildings are. the Northern border is drawn from the Mordmar Falls to the Wester Estuary of Tyree Lake.

## River Ward

Gateway to Tinnia and the northeast. There is a reserviour and a series of lockes to the west where boards can rise to the reservior, or lower to the bay: the Tyree Lockes.
The NE region is a series of marshes and river channels that allow farming and freshwater fishing.

## Stannum Ward

Metalurgy and Earthen crafts are made here. Mostly Dwarves, Goliaths, metalwork, forging, underdark items.
Source of ore and small forge operations (weapons, armor, craftsmen). If you want large forging and casting metalwork, go west across the bay to the Ferrion ward.

## Aurus Ward

Wood and lumber. Controversial area because faithful wood elves resent sea elves making a living doing forestry and carpentry.
This is the source of the majority of lumber for the coast. Much resentment to Dakkon for undercutting the lumber market.

## Gnishra Ward

Civilian hobs, half-hobs, orcs, half-orcs, goblins. This region is where the southern midland highway lands from 
the eastern desert and the highlands northeast of NE Burati valley. Expect to be shaken down.
The crime bosses will defend the area from attack, and were an unrecognized first defense vs Yuan'ti infiltrators.

## Bismount Ward

This is where the coal mines and the smelters run.
All are welcome, few will endure.
Forge bosses are renound for press-ganging people from the coastal highway and sailors from both shores to work coal and iron.

## Coal Trench

This is both the garden and the closet of the Bismount coal barons. To the north: a firing range and combat proving ground for the navy.
Centuries of mining coal have left the area much closer to the sea level, and flooded mine shafts dot the valley. 

## Ferrion Ward

Home of the Sing'jar military: naval yards, marine barracks, the harbormaster office, the coastal guard. 
As you go south, the military industrial complex builds the large items of war: cannons, sheet metal and 
large gears are cast--all fueled by the coal trench. Cannon, rifles, anchors, chains are produced here and sold 
all around the bay. If you want craftsmanship, go east to the Stannum ward.
