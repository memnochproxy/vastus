Title: Scatznex Docs Magical Emergency

Setting:
    Scatznex docs, southwest Sing'jar, close to the coal shipping terminals.
    People are gray with coal coke residue, most are covering their mouths with
    cloths. There are old gnomes, some old dwarves, and adolescent humans
    dragging sleds of minerals from iron-wheeled "big rigged carts" that
    deliver mine ore.

Big intro: 23
    Jaytz Keltcash, neighborhood police officer, in uniform, runs from the
    bakery into the plaza and whips out a wand. It casts harsh light and shadows
    across Jaytz face while he deploys the magic weapon. The wand punches a
    black hole in the sky and the flame around it continues to burn the
    facing buildings. You see a suddently flaming beam crack and fall on
    Deputy Dz'mutz!

Scene secrets:
    Deputy Dz'murtz is an agent a Yuan'ti cell from eastern Witkif.
    Jaytz Keltcash is a 11th level Asimar cleric.
    The item that created a black hole was not the wand but an
    enchanted bath of speculation holding a captive Naga ghost
