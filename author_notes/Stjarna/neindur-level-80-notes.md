## Neindur Level 80

This level is roughly level to the dry shor4e. There are no captives above level 75. The cells between lv30 and lv80 are just families of birds nesting in skeletons glues to the walls by nests and guano. Sound is muted, you hear no echos despite the geometrical perfection of the walls. The bustling breeze above is entirely forgotten. You blink at the drafts of piquent ammonia and mold drafting up from the depths of the giant pit.

Level 80 is convenient because only levels 1, 40, 80 and 120 were build w any type of a balcony or deck. As you alight on the deck of level 80, a company of archers leans out of the shadows, and the rustle of their action is carried far around the pit...maybe a full company of forest elves? They have silver neck bracers and all of them are scrutinizing you. They relax their long bows an a slight breeze sweeps away the existential tension. ## Neindur Level 100
__The subterranian entrance__

As you exit the tunnel, there have not been barnicles for hundreds of feet since you began. The briney smell has long disappeared and now what you smell is very similar to a rotten log pried up from the forest floor. The doorway to the shaft only has a path about 20 inches wide, and is wet with drizzle and moss grows between the stones. The light filtering down is dim shafts of almost vertical light, wavering from the slowly moving foliage above. You see no blue sky, you see an unclimbably high, lichen and alge covered stone pit obscured by something dangling steamers of moss that you know is not tree branches. Ten feet above you is the crumpled frame of an airship, its gas envelope now slowly swaying with moss tracers, dripping watter on everything below. After about five heartbeats, a breeze rolls down the shaft and you hear the scratching of metal on stone: rutrut-rut-rutrut--rut. Every sway of the old airframe releases a shower of dew.

* Perception 11: there is no gondola on the airframe and the structure is bent in the middle like a "V". It does not look like animals feel safe nesting on this.

* Perception 13: snakes! Why did it have to be Snakes!

* Perception 16: there are moss covered craters in the walls with rusty bolts sticking out

* Perception 19: the moss obscures skeletons of victims slammed so hard against the wall by rebar-thick staples about 5 inches wide that only the spine and thorax of the victim remains to feed the moss. 

## Neindur Level 120
The path you climb down to is smells like warm swamp goo with a hint of sulphur and despair. The beams of light casting down from the mossy complex above are sparse and narrow. The water pool inches away is black and rippling with the drops of dew cast from the moss above. You kick a bent piece of metal: a bronze cylinder with magic runes, it rolls into the drink. Stepping backwards your calf is scratched by a foot long, five inch wide rusty iron staple, one prong stuck into the stone, the other bent askew. After taking a few halting breaths, you notice the walls have mildly luminescent steaks of...slime? Glancing around you see much evidence of something resembling a century of torture: barely articulated arms and legs are pinned to the walls with large rusty staples. Piles of bones mixed with glowing ooze and moss sit below each of these small shattered areas holding bits of victims.
